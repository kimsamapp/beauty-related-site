What is Hilot massage?

Hilot is a traditional Filipino healing massage that uses warm oil, gentle pressure and stretching to ease tension and improve circulation.

Is prenatal massage safe?

It is usually fine after the first trimester. Please check with your doctor first and tell me about your pregnancy when you book.
